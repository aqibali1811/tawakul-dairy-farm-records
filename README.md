## Project 1: Mastitis Analysis - 20 Cows
**File:** `Tawakul_Mastitis_Record_20cows_Tawakul_Style.xlsx`

- Dairy farm mastitis records: TAG #, Breed, Milk Yield, SCC, CMT, Treatment
- **Analysis in Python:**
  - Average SCC of mastitis cows (SCC > 200k): 584.0 x 1000 cells/ml
  - Average SCC of healthy cows: 100.0 x 1000
  - Milk loss due to mastitis: 8.2 Liters per cow per day
  - 10 mastitis cases out of 20
  - Chronic repeaters identified

**Python Code:** `tawakul_mastitis_analysis_TawakulStyle.py`
